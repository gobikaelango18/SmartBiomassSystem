✅ Quality Assessment

A batch passes only when all three measured parameters satisfy the entered limits.

  • Moisture must not exceed the specified maximum.
  • GCV must meet or exceed the specified minimum.
  • Ash content must not exceed the specified maximum.

These are input-based checks, not verified laboratory results. Use the actual buyer specification for a real order.
